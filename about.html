About the Artist

The world is not simple; it is complicated—beautiful and dirty, contrasting and invigorating, sometimes confrontational and sometimes underwhelming in its predictability. People mingle yet remain solitary, walking together while unaware of what surrounds them. Old buildings stand beside new, high-rises cast shadows over historic landmarks, and light plays against dark. Powerful and inconsequential figures alike move through these spaces, all part of the same shared composition.

Stephen Sironi paints life as he sees it, capturing quiet snapshots of everyday experience and the layered complexity of urban and communal spaces. His work reflects the tension between connection and isolation, permanence and change, ornament and geometry—moments that are at once ordinary and deeply human.

Born and raised just south of Boston, Steve came of age during an era of artistic experimentation, discovery, and cultural turmoil. He earned a degree in Fine Arts from the University of Massachusetts Amherst and has lived a life rooted in creativity and artistic expression. He currently resides in central Ohio.
